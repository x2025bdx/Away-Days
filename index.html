THE BUSINESS IDEA

Useful first. Revenue second.

Earn trust with a genuinely helpful free product, then charge for extra convenience.

OK

RECOMMENDATION

Skip ads at launch. Test a paid upgrade.

Watering reminders are a quick, personal task. Ads would interrupt the habit and likely earn little before the app has a large, engaged audience.

01

Free care basics

Keep plant tracking and watering priorities free. That makes it easy to try and gives people a reason to return.

The habit is the product's foundation.

02 - BEST FIRST TEST

Optional Tend Plus

Test a modest subscription for shared household care, care history, vacation plans, and more detailed plant guides.

Validate willingness to pay before building it.

03 - LATER

Plant sitters

A local sitter marketplace could take a booking fee, especially for trips. Start by testing demand in one community.

Trust, availability, support, and safe payments make this much harder.

This is a student prototype. Upgrade purchases and sitter bookings are ideas only; no payments or bookings are processed.
